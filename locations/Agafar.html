Lore

Carved directly into the jagged, sun-baked peaks of the desert biome, Agafar stands as a majestic yet vulnerable testament to old desert architecture. An ancient sandstone city where the protective barriers of the past have crumbled into dust.

The Mountain Sanctuary

Rising steeply through the rugged desert mountains, Agafar was built centuries ago as a secluded, high-altitude refuge. Constructed entirely from local sandstone, the city seamlessly blends into the surrounding cliffs, its multi-tiered residential quarters cascading down the rocky slopes. At the absolute heart of this ancient Arabic settlement sits a grand, domed mosque flanked by twin towering minarets, serving as a historic beacon of community and faith for the mountain folk.

For generations, the city's sheer elevation and a formidable outer fortification wall kept it safe from both wandering raiders and the early tremors of the mainland's collapse.

Traces of Erosion

  • The magnificent central mosque, its stone dome and high windows still watching over the silent plazas below.
  • The vast network of tightly packed sandstone houses and terrace buildings, slowly being swallowed by encroaching desert sands.
  • The complete absence of the outer fortified wall, which long ago collapsed down the mountain face, leaving the inner city entirely exposed to the elements.

The Crumbled Defense

Agafar’s downfall was not a sudden siege, but a slow, structural betrayal. The shifting mountain earth and relentless desert winds gradually undermined the foundation of the outer fortified walls. When the protective barrier finally gave way and tumbled into the ravines, the city lost its only line of defense. Exposed and vulnerable to the dangers lurking in the valleys below, the citizens gradually abandoned their mountain homes, leaving the gorgeous sandstone architecture to weather the centuries alone.

Present Day

Today, the silent streets of Agafar offer an excellent destination for mid-tier explorers transitioning out of the early game. Because the city was a thriving mountain community rather than a military outpost, the remaining civilian chests, domestic cupboards, and abandoned market stalls are filled with a reliable mix of low- to medium-tier loot. Travelers can find upgraded survival gear, durable desert attire, and useful crafting materials tucked away inside the multi-story sandstone dwellings.

Scale the winding mountain paths to uncover the historic heart of Agafar. Watch your footing among the cliffside residential tiers, for without the ancient outer walls, a single misstep can send you tumbling into the desert abyss.
