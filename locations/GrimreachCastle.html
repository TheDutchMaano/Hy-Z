Grimreach Castle

Grimreach Castle was once the proud of the south. Located just below the mountain range and north from the swamplands, defending this behemoth of a castle was supposed to be easy, with its giant moat giving a huge advantage to the defenders. The old catacombs that are buried below the castle are notoriously hard to navigate. Despite all the advantages the defenders had, the castle eventually got overrun as too many undead came simultaneously. The castle is now a dangerous place to explore, but it is also one of the best early game locations in terms of loot.

Lore

Once the proud crown jewel of the realm's defenses, Grimreach Castle now stands as a dark, silent monolith of decay: a fallen stronghold completely overrun by the undead.

The Sovereign Shield

For centuries, Grimreach Castle was an impenetrable fortress of stone and massive oak beams, acting as the primary guardian of the valley and its surrounding lands. Its grand halls, vast dining quarters, and heavily fortified stone corridors were built to withstand generations of warfare, serving as a beacon of safety and military power for the entire kingdom.

The castle's defenses were meticulously designed as a multi-layered network of interconnected stone structures, allowing its garrison to lock down entire wings at a moment's notice. It was a place of strategic brilliance, housing vast stockpiles of weaponry, deep storage vaults, and grand meeting chambers where the realm's protectors coordinated the defense of the territory.

The Night of the Siege

  • The sudden, overwhelming surge of undead forces that swarmed the lower valley and choked the castle gates.
  • The frantic fallback into the secondary wings as the outer courtyard walls were completely breached.
  • The ultimate compromise of the main corridors, forcing the surviving defenders to abandon their home entirely.

A Kingdom Extinguished

The fall of Grimreach shattered the region's stability. When the undead breached the inner sanctums, the iron-clad defense collapsed into a desperate, chaotic retreat, driving the remaining soldiers to flee into the anonymity of the nearby mountains. The grand architectural masterclass that once shielded humanity was instantly transformed into a massive, twisted spawning ground for corrupted creatures.

Present Day

Today, Grimreach Castle is a hollowed-out labyrinth of shadows and cobwebs, haunted by the very creatures that brought it down. Its once-glorious halls are now silent, save for the dragging steps of the undead. Despite the extreme danger, the castle remains a prime target for daring plunderers, as its massive storage rooms, abandoned armories, and elite quarters still hold some of the highest-value military loot and forgotten royal relics in the realm—if one can navigate the dark corridors and make it out alive.

To gaze upon the silhouette of Grimreach Castle is to look at the grave of a kingdom. Approach with absolute stealth: the monsters that claimed these stone walls have no intention of letting their trophies go.
