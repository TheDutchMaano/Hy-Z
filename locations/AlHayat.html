Lore

Clinging to the scorching eastern coast where the desert meets the sea, the fortified city of Al-Hayat stands as a hollow, sun-bleached tomb—a place whose name ironically promises "Life," but now holds only the echoing silence of a self-inflicted doom.

The Wealth of the Crypts

In its golden age, Al-Hayat was a prosperous, bustling Arabic coastal city protected by sweeping stone walls and a heavily guarded harbor. The foundation of its immense wealth, however, was built on sacrilege. The merchants and raiders of the city became deeply invested in the forbidden Scarab culture of the deep desert, routinely plundering ancient inland crypts for hidden treasures.

Their most lucrative and dangerous commodities were the giant scarab eggs buried deep within the tombs. Worth a hefty fortune to scholars, alchemists, and wealthy collectors across the realm, these eggs became the lifeblood of Al-Hayat's economy. To safely export them, the city’s apothecaries discovered a specialized chemical mixture; by meticulously spraying the shells, they could keep the highly volatile scarab embryos in a permanently dormant state indefinitely.

The Great Oversight

  • The grand market stalls and seaside docks, once overflowing with gold gained from the high-priced dormant egg trade.
  • A single, catastrophic mistake in the storage vaults, where a freshly harvested egg was accidentally forgotten and left unsprayed.
  • The sudden hatching of a monstrous, ravenous scarab that quickly grew into an unstoppable apex predator within the city streets.

The Locking of the Gates

The hatched nightmare tore through the civilian districts with terrifying speed, decimating most of the population in a matter of hours. As total annihilation loomed, a brave, forgotten soldier made the ultimate sacrifice. In a desperate final act, he lured the beast into the outer defensive ring and manually locked the massive, reinforced fortified walls from the inside. He lost his life to the creature, but his bravery successfully contained the monstrosity within the city's perimeter, saving the few survivors trapped outside.

Present Day

With their wealth shattered and their homes turned into a prison for a monster, the handful of surviving citizens had no choice but to vacate the area completely and scatter into the desert wasteland. Today, Al-Hayat is entirely abandoned by humanity. Wandering scavengers who brave the eastern shores can still find lucrative civilian chests, old merchant stockpiles, and valuable starting loot left intact within the coastal houses—provided they keep their distance from the central fortifications where the ancient horror still waits.

Explore the sun-drenched docks of Al-Hayat to claim the spoils of a fallen empire. But heed the warning etched into the sand: do not disturb the locked walls, for some prices are far too high for a chest of gold.
