The Smelly Fish

The Smelly Fish is the name given to the Inn in a small fishing village on the south coast. It's built on wooden poles inside the water. It has some houses, a small boat, cases for fishing (now used to trap undead), and most notably ofcourse, it has an Inn in the middle of the town called 'The Smelly Fish'.

Lore

Clinging to the murky, disease-ridden waters of the wetlands stand the rotting remains of The Smelly Fish: a tragic monument to human stubbornness that, despite its ingenious defenses, ultimately drowned in the undead tide.

The Stilt Settlement

Before the world fell apart, this nameless hamlet was nothing more than a quiet cluster of wooden houses suspended over the water, home to a tight-knit community of fishermen. When the outbreak swept across the mainland, the villagers believed the deep water would act as a natural moat. By severing connections to the shore and expanding their network of floating docks, they turned their vulnerable village into a floating fortress, using small rowboats to gather resources under the cover of night.

For a time, the gamble worked. But isolation bred complacency, and the very waters that protected them eventually became a highway for the eternal, un-drowning horde that slowly eroded the settlement's foundations.

Desperate Defenses

  • The heavy wooden crates, initially repurposed to trap and contain aquatic undead drifting through the currents.
  • The water-logged traps that eventually became overloaded, snapping under the weight of too many monsters.
  • The rickety walkways, now broken and blood-stained, where the final, chaotic bottleneck took place.

The Fall of the Fish

The end did not come with a breach of the gates, but with a silent, relentless overflow. The trapped dead in the fishing cages drew more of their kind, until the waters beneath the stilts were teeming with teeth and rotting flesh. One catastrophic night, the wooden poles groaned and gave way under the sheer mass of the climbing infected, sending the defensive perimeter crumbling into the swamp.

The Last Innkeeper

The undeniable heart of the settlement was the prominent, multi-story tavern standing dead center in the town, proudly bearing its glowing green sign: "The Smelly Fish". It was inside this mossy building that the final stand occurred. Today, the tavern is a silent tomb. Upstairs, in the main quarters, the Inn's owner lies dead in his bed—having refused to flee or abandon his life's work, choosing instead to go down with his tavern like a captain staying aboard his sinking ship.

Present Day

Now, The Smelly Fish is completely overrun, a ghost town rotting away on the water. The glowing green sign still flickers mockingly in the swamp mist, but the only remaining residents are the dripping, water-logged dead wandering the planks. Adventurers brave enough to clear out the groaning structures can still find leftover fishing loot and the untouched vault of the loyal Innkeeper—assuming they don't join the bodies floating beneath the docks.

To tread the damp planks of The Smelly Fish is to walk through a cautionary tale of false security. Keep your weapons drawn: the village has completely fallen, and the things waiting in the shadows are very hungry.
