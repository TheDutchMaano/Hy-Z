Lore

Perched as a haunting silhouette of old-world aristocracy, Chateau Valemont stands in ruin: a once-magnificent four-story estate where luxury and devotion have been crushed by collapsing stone and the unyielding march of the dead.

The Seat of Alsatian

In the golden days of the realm, Chateau Valemont was the private, luxurious estate of Baron and Baroness Alsatian. Famed for its elegant architecture and a towering spire that overlooked the surrounding valleys, the massive four-story chateau was a symbol of high society, wealth, and unchecked privilege. Its grand halls were once filled with fine art, rich tapestries, and the elite of the southern provinces.

The estate was entirely self-sufficient, even featuring its own grand church built directly into the heart of the complex to serve the family and their guests. However, opulence offered no protection when the world fractured. Isolated from major military hubs, the mansion became an easy target for early waves of the undead, and its high walls quickly turned from a peaceful retreat into a wealthy tomb.

Traces of Ruin

  • The iconic central tower, now split open and partially collapsed directly into the main estate.
  • The grand roof, shattered by falling masonry, with heavy stone debris still littering the floors below.
  • The private estate church, its holy altars and pews now silent, decaying, and desecrated by the infected.

The Overgrown Guestrooms

Across its four sprawling levels, the chateau housed numerous lavish guestrooms meant for visiting nobility. Today, these rooms are a macabre shadow of their former grandeur. Nature has forced its way through the shattered windows, leaving the quarters completely overgrown with creeping vines and choked by thick layers of dusty spiderwebs. The heavy oak doors rattle in the wind, hiding the restless spirits of those who checked in and never left.

The Shattered Spire

The definitive blow to the chateau came during the final days of its defense. As the undead swarmed the lower levels, the structural integrity of the grand tower failed entirely. The catastrophic collapse caved in the upper roof, pancaking the structure into the levels underneath. This violent destruction effectively sealed off entire wings of the mansion, trapping both the remaining infected and the Baron's assets beneath tons of jagged rock and splintered wood.

Present Day

Today, Chateau Valemont is a decaying labyrinth of shadows, fractured stone, and creeping rot. While the narrative of the Alsatian family is dark, the estate serves as an excellent hunting ground for newly arrived scavengers. Because it was primarily a residential property rather than a military fort, players can safely scour the overgrown guestrooms and the broken church benches for accessible early-game loot, basic clothing items, leftover food supplies, and standard civilian chests.

To explore the four stories of Chateau Valemont is to walk among the ruins of broken nobility and fallen faith. Tread lightly when looting the guestrooms: the debris below is a grim reminder that the ceiling can give way at any moment.
