Lore

Drifting like silent, glowing stars above the arid dunes, The Balloons cast an eerie, permanent light over the southern part of the desert region. A fleet of hot-air balloons, ironically, frozen in time following a rescue mission gone wrong.

The Voyage From Beyond

According to ancient stellar charts and rumors whispered among desert nomads, this majestic fleet of airships did not originate from this realm. It is widely believed they were constructed and sent from a world far away, explicitly commissioned for a single, high-profile task: to retrieve the powerful eccentric wizard residing in the nearby Eye Spy Tower.

The extraction itself was seemingly successful, and the fleet turned back toward the heavens, charting a course back to their distant home across the desert expanse. However, they would never leave the atmosphere.

The Call of the Crash

  • The sudden, unexplained failure of a primary airship, causing one of the massive balloons to spiral downward and crash violently into the desert sand below.
  • The immediate anchoring of the rest of the fleet as the crew initiated a frantic rescue and recovery operation for the downed vessel.
  • The sudden arrival of an unknown, malevolent force that swiftly and utterly decimated every single living soul within the fleet during the rescue attempt.

The Eternal Drift

Though the crew was entirely wiped out in a matter of moments, the airships themselves remained remarkably intact. The vessels were powered by a rare, mystical "eternal fire"—an arcane fuel source that never goes out and requires no tending. Because the magical flames continue to burn at a perfectly constant temperature, the remaining balloons have stayed afloat for centuries, frozen in mid-air above the dunes, completely unaffected by the passing desert storms.

Present Day

For travelers navigating the southern desert, the glowing silhouette of the fleet serves as both a landmark and a high-stakes playground. The lower decks and dangling ropes of the closer vessels offer accessible starter loot, basic survival gear, and old traveler supplies left behind by the vanished crew.

However, the true prize lies much higher. Daring adventurers can attempt to scale the rigging and navigate the treacherous, wind-swept ropes. By completing a perilous sequence of high-altitude parkour across the hulls of the frozen fleet, players can reach a hidden, rare chest packed with valuable old-world artifacts—just don't look down.

To look up at The Balloons is to witness a rescue mission frozen in eternity. Step carefully if you choose to climb into the sky: the unknown force that cleared these decks may be gone, but gravity is just as unforgiving.
