Lore

Suspended high above the dark, shadow-drenched floor of the primeval jungle lies The Treacherous Treehouse: a sprawling, moss-covered canopy fortress that stands as a brilliant monument to survival, stubbornness, and a single man's war against the dead.

The Canopy of Greenreach

The locals once called the great canopy settlement Greenreach, built high among the ancient trees where the forest floor could not threaten what was above. Constructed out of rich timber and connected by winding rope bridges and wooden platforms, it was a peaceful, isolated haven for those who sought to live in absolute harmony with nature. When the zombies came—slow at first, then unstoppable—the people fled upward, believing height and wood would keep the dead below. Most eventually abandoned the place entirely. All but one.

Arlen, the Last Warden

The man who remains is known as Arlen, the Last Warden. Before the dead ever walked these woods, he served as one of Greenreach’s guardians—one of the quiet rangers who kept watch over the people living in the forest. When the outbreak reached the settlement and the others fled for safer lands, Arlen stayed behind. He turned the treehouse into a defensive stronghold, sealing ladders, reinforcing platforms, and converting the narrow bridges into deadly choke points. The cages, spike pits, and hanging traps scattered through the trunks were all his doing, meant to slow the endless climb of the undead. What had once been a peaceful lookout among the leaves became a battlefield suspended in the branches.

Defensive Installations

  • The fortified wooden towers built straight into the massive tree trunks, keeping the high ground secure.
  • The narrow, torch-lit sky bridges designed to bottle up and funnel incoming threats into Arlen's line of fire.
  • The heavy swinging traps and dropping cages hidden expertly within the thick jungle foliage to catch climbers unaware.

The Eternal Vigil

No one knows how long Arlen has held the fortress in the trees. From the canopy he keeps constant vigil, watching the forest floor through the leaves and broken walkways, loosing shots and triggering traps when the dead begin their slow climb up the trunks. The strange part is that they never wander far. Night after night the zombies return to the same trees, drawn again and again to the lantern light and the lone defender above them.

Arlen seems to understand this grim pattern, because he never abandons the treehouse—not even when escape would be easy. As long as he holds this perch, the dead remain here, clawing endlessly at the bark instead of spreading deeper into the forest. Whether he fights out of stubborn duty, guilt for those who fled, or simply because he refuses to surrender the last safe place in the forest, Greenreach is a mystery whispered by the few travelers who have glimpsed the fires burning high in the leaves.

Present Day

For players navigating the dense jungle biome, reaching the Treehouse is an incredible, tier-appropriate milestone. The lower branches and perimeter platforms offer excellent low- to mid-tier scout armor, standard hunting bows, and civilian chests left behind by the fleeing villagers. For those skilled enough to parkour up the vertical pathways and bypass Arlen's active traps, the master Warden's overlook contains pristine survival tools and heavy ammunition caches—valuable loot for any adventurer looking to tame the jungle.

Look up into the canopy to find the flickering warmth of Greenreach. Tread carefully on the wooden planks: a step out of place means a long drop into a dark jungle floor where the climbing dead wait for a warden to fall.
