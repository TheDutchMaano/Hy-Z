Lore

Standing as the final, desperate stronghold against the frozen wastes of the north, there lies Enoch: once a grand bastion of unyielding faith, now a fractured haven ticking closer to total collapse.

The Northern Refuge

In ages past, Enoch was a beacon of civilization in the bitter cold, strong in its religious devotion and fiercely united beneath towering stone spires. But as the zombie infection ravaged the continent and external societies crumbled, the city's massive gates became a curse. A sudden, overwhelming influx of desperate refugees flooded the outer wards, straining the citadel's resources to a breaking point and fracturing the social order.

The true downfall of Enoch did not come from the hordes clawing at the walls, but from the rot within. As fear outgrew faith, starving citizens and desperate newcomers turned away from their gods, giving rise to hidden cults, heretics, and the practice of forbidden dark magic.

Signs of Decay

  • The severe overcrowding and starvation tearing through the tightly clustered medieval housing.
  • The Great Cathedral's looming shadow, where citizens now trade holy prayers for heretical rituals.
  • The spreading madness fueled by dark magic, driving the population into violent insanity.

The Looming Collapse

The dark energy seeping through the cobblestone streets has completely dismantled the law and order that once defined the northern capital. Paranoia rules the narrow corridors and defensive choke points. Enoch was meticulously built to withstand an external siege for centuries, yet the internal corruption ensures that the city's grand walls will serve as its own tomb before the winter ends.

Present Day

The inner anarchy has turned the fortified sanctuary into a labyrinth of terror. While the grand architecture still stands, the streets are now haunted by maddened zealots and the rising dead. Yet, daring explorers still risk the frozen journey to Enoch, hunting for the high-end loot and ancient religious relics left behind in the abandoned manors, navigating a dying city that will not stand much longer.

To cross into Enoch's gates is to walk the thin line between surviving the apocalypse and succumbing to the madness within. Proceed with caution: the city is fracturing, and the dark magic is only a ritual away from tearing the last holdout apart.
