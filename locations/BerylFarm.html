Lore

Nestled along the main path on the southern coastline, Beryl Farm stands as a silent, decaying homestead. A peaceful agricultural sanctuary that was quickly abandoned when the horrors of the infected reached its fields.

The Fertile Fields

Before the collapse, Beryl Farm was a bustling and prosperous estate, responsible for supplying fresh produce and livestock to the surrounding settlements. Its sprawling, neatly segmented crop patches and sturdy wooden barns were a testament to the family's hard work. Strategically located in the fertile lowlands, it served as a vital rest stop for travelers looking to trade or rest before making the long journey toward the harsher northern territories.

Because it was primarily a peaceful homestead, the farm lacks major fortifications, making it an easy target when the infection began to bleed out from the fallen cities.

The Sickness in the Pens

  • The livestock pens, where the farm's prize pigs tragically contracted the zombie infection, turning violent and rabid.
  • The makeshift slaughter, where the heartbroken farmer managed to put down his own infected animals before the sickness could spread further.
  • The withered, dead crops rotting in their tilled soil plots, completely abandoned to the elements.

A Hasty Retreat

Though the farmer successfully contained the infected livestock, the encounter made it clear that the farm's flimsy wooden fences could not withstand a true horde. Realizing they were completely defenseless, the family packed what little they could carry and fled elsewhere into the wilderness. They left behind their life's work, allowing the untended crops to rot and turning the quiet pastures into a roaming ground where the wandering undead now thrive.

Present Day

Today, Beryl Farm is a highly valuable pitstop for newly arrived adventurers looking to find their footing. Because the family fled in a panic, they left behind scattered civilian chests containing useful early-game gear, basic tools, and leftover food supplies in the main farmhouse. Most importantly, the property features a clean water well that remains completely uncontaminated. It is the perfect place to safely fill up your water bottles, catch your breath, and stock your inventory before pushing further north into more dangerous territories.

Scavenge the tool sheds of Beryl Farm to build up your basic inventory. Just keep an eye on the rotting fields: the farmer may have left, but the things wandering between the dead crops are always looking for a fresh meal.
