The Great Retreat

The origin of this secret redoubt lies in the tragic fall of nearby Grimreach Castle. When the relentless hordes of the undead breached the castle's towering walls and overran its defenses, the surviving soldiers and citizens were forced to flee into the harsh, vertical wilderness. Knowing they could not survive a conventional siege in the open, they retreated into the mountains to establish a hidden staging ground.

Carved silently out of natural stone caverns, the base was engineered for absolute security. By using the natural geography to their advantage, the survivors successfully built a subterranean fortress capable of sustaining a guerilla war against the corrupted army occupying their former home.

Defensive Measures

  • The invisible entrance, completely absent from regional maps and masked by overgrown mountain brush.
  • Heavy iron bars and reinforced grates fortifying every internal choke point and cave mouth.
  • Strategic guard scaffolding overlooking the lower shafts to thin out any creeping undead from absolute safety.

The Coiled Spring

Living in the literal shadow of a fallen kingdom means that paranoia and vigilance rule the dim, torch-lit corridors. Every sector of the headquarters is built for functionality, from the secure fallback points in the deep mine-works to the tactical rooms where scouts track enemy movements in the valley below. The iron gates only open for those who bring steel to the cause, ensuring the base remains a ghost to the world outside.

Present Day

Today, the Resistance HQ stands as a powerful symbol of hope and resilience in a world overrun by darkness. While the undead roam free across the lowlands, the last bastion of humanity holds the line within these subterranean halls. Daring explorers and loyal allies who successfully navigate the treacherous mountain paths can find high-value military loot and a safe haven to trade—provided they share the resolve to strike back.
