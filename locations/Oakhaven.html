Lore

Once the beating heart of maritime trade and the undisputed crown jewel of the meridian shores, Oakhaven now stands as a colossal, hollowed-out metropolis. A grand harbor city strangled by dark corruption and buried under a glowing blanket of parasitic fungi.

The Sovereign Port

As the largest city on the southern part of the map and a central trade hub, Oakhaven was a marvel of medieval engineering. Its sprawling docks once welcomed merchant fleets from every corner of the realm, while its towering, giant stone walls were deemed utterly impenetrable. Behind those mighty fortifications laid thriving markets, grand stone manors, and bustling civilian districts that promised absolute safety to all who lived within.

But when the world fractured, Oakhaven’s size became its undoing. The very walls built to keep the nightmares out turned the city into a massive, claustrophobic trap when the corruption took root from the inside, leaving the vast civilian population with nowhere to run.

The Neon Rot

  • The massive stone ramparts and streets, now completely choked and cracked by overgrown, bioluminescent fungi.
  • The haunting, eerie glow of the fungal spores that illuminate the abandoned alleys in a sickly green and blue light.
  • The residual civilian stockpiles—basic early-game loot, scattered food rations, and old household chests left behind in the panic.

The Subterranean Tomb

When the upper streets fell to the infected hordes, thousands of terrified citizens fled beneath the pavement into Oakhaven's famously elaborate sewer system. Designed to handle the waste of a metropolis, the under-city was a dizzying, multi-layered labyrinth of stone aqueducts and vaulted tunnels. It was meant to be a temporary shelter, but it quickly transformed into a mass grave. Without maps or light, the fleeing survivors succumbed to starvation and madness, utterly unable to find their way out of the pitch-black, twisting maze.

The Broodmother's End

The survivors weren't the only ones who claimed the dark depths. Deep within the central drainage vault lies the massive, rotting carcass of a giant arachnid that once hunted the lost citizens. In a final, desperate act of heroism, a forgotten defender managed to slay the monster, piercing a wooden spike straight through its head. Though the beast lies dead, its webs still choke the tunnels, serving as a grim warning of what used to rule the dark.

Present Day

Today, the surface of Oakhaven is a prime looting ground for newly arrived adventurers. Because it was a civilian city, the ruins are highly accessible, offering plenty of basic supplies, simple tools, and forgotten satchels scattered across the fungal-encrusted harbor. The streets are dangerous but manageable for those cautious enough to avoid the lingering infected wandering the docks.

Scavenge the sunlit harbor of Oakhaven for easy spoils, but heed this absolute warning: do not, under any circumstances, venture into the sewers. If the glowing rot doesn't claim you, the endless, blind turns of the labyrinth will ensure you never see the sky again.
